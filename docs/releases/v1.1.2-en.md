# Ocean Shallows v1.1.2

[中文](v1.1.2.md)

Fixed the dolphin appearing to vanish during re-entry and then pop back into view. Previously, the underwater portion had a fixed 12% opacity, causing an abrupt drop as the body crossed the waterline. It now retains pink color, shaded volume and a teal water tint, with opacity varying smoothly by depth. The submerged silhouette remains visible.

The full sequence now lasts about 2.8 seconds. Takeoff and flight keep their pace, while the underwater swim and recovery have more time. The final 0.65 seconds blend smoothly into the normal swimming pose while aligning the body position. Protected chat controls and surface ripple behavior are unchanged.

## Download and update

- `Ocean-Shallows-v1.1.2-macOS.zip`
- `Ocean-Shallows-v1.1.2-Windows.zip`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

Back up the active engine resources. Copy `assets/renderer-inject.js` and the matching platform stylesheet into the same engine `assets` folder. On Windows, use `assets/windows/dream-skin.css` and name the destination `dream-skin.css`. Reapply **Ocean Shallows Live**. Compatible with Dream Skin 1.5.21.

For first installation and folder locations, see the [README](https://github.com/Asyou-GD/codex_ocean/tree/v1.1.2#找到软件实际使用的文件夹) and [Windows guide](https://github.com/Asyou-GD/codex_ocean/blob/v1.1.2/docs/windows.md).
